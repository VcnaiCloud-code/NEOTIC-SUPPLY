CHARACTER MOTION:

Give each character a slightly different motion personality.

NEO:
- Slight vertical movement.
- Very subtle horizontal drift.
- Slightly more unpredictable than the others.
- Tiny scale/depth variation.

VEX:
- Slow, smooth floating movement.
- Slight lateral drift.
- Softer motion than NEO.

RAZE:
- Very subtle depth movement.
- Almost static compared with the others.
- Slight parallax response to mouse movement.

MIKO:
- Smooth floating movement.
- Slight vertical movement.
- Slightly more playful but still premium.

IMPORTANT:
These movements must be extremely subtle.
The characters should never look like they are bouncing.
They should feel like they are suspended in space.

MOUSE PARALLAX:

Create a layered mouse-parallax system.

When the mouse moves:
- Background moves very slightly.
- Characters move at different depth levels.
- Logo moves minimally.
- Decorative elements move slightly more.
- Foreground elements move slightly less or more depending on depth.

Use smooth interpolation / easing.
Never snap directly to the mouse position.

The effect should feel like cinematic camera movement rather than a website effect.

SCROLL PARALLAX:

As the user scrolls away from the hero:
- Characters should slowly move at different speeds.
- Hero content should gradually transition out.
- The next section should begin appearing underneath.
- Use opacity and transform transitions.
- Avoid sudden disappearance.

The transition should feel like entering another environment.
